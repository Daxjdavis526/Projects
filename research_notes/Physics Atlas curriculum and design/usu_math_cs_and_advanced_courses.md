## 3. How MATH 2250 relates to MATH 2270 + MATH 2280 in this program

### Takeaway
The degree map presents them as alternatives: "MATH 2250 or MATH 2270 /MATH 2280 must be taken before PHYS 3750". The program names MATH 2280 as its highest math requirement. That suggests the 2270 + 2280 pair is the listed default and the 4-credit MATH 2250 an accepted substitute. The exact wording of the requirement block was not visible.
- **MATH 2250** compresses linear algebra and ODEs into one 4-credit course, which USU describes as engineering-oriented.
- **MATH 2280** alone is 3 credits. Its description adds boundary value problems, which the MATH 2250 description does not mention.

### Cited Findings
- **Degree map:** "MATH 2250 or MATH 2270 /MATH 2280 must be taken before PHYS 3750, fall of junior year." — [4 Year Degree Map (catoid 39)](https://catalog.usu.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=39&poid=40015)
- **Highest math requirement:** "The highest math requirement for this degree is MATH 2280: Ordinary Differential Equations (QI)." — [Program page (catoid 40)](https://catalog.usu.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=40&poid=40466&returnto=30826)
- **MATH 2250** (4 cr, QI): linear systems; abstract vector spaces; matrices through eigenvalues and eigenvectors; solutions of ODEs; Laplace transforms; first-order systems. — [catalog.usu.edu/courses/MATH2250](https://catalog.usu.edu/courses/MATH2250). The AMLC calls it "primarily oriented towards students in engineering." — [AMLC MATH 2250](https://artsci.usu.edu/math-stats/amlc/course-materials/math-2250/index.php)
- **MATH 2280** (3 cr, QI): analytic ODE solution techniques; initial *and boundary* value problems with applications; higher-order scalar equations; first-order linear systems; Laplace transforms. — [catalog.usu.edu/courses/MATH2280](https://catalog.usu.edu/courses/MATH2280)
- **MATH 2270** "Linear Algebra" is active (Spring 2026 recitations). Its official description was not retrieved. — [AMLC recitation leaders](https://artsci.usu.edu/math-stats/amlc/recitation/leaders/index)
- **Historical evidence:**
  - The department's old requirements page listed MATH 2250 (4 cr) as the BS "Other Mathematics" requirement. — [physics.usu.edu/core.html](https://www.physics.usu.edu/core.html)
  - The Fall 2016 PHYS 3750 syllabus lists the prerequisites as "Phyx 2710, Math 2210; Math 2250 (may be taken concurrently)". — [PHYS 3750 syllabus 2016](https://www.physics.usu.edu/riffe/3750/Syllabus%203750%202016.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Both routes count for the PHYS 3750 sequencing rule.**
- **The 2270 + 2280 route is deeper:** 6 credits if MATH 2270 is 3, against 4 for MATH 2250. It gives a full semester of linear algebra (bases, linear maps, eigen-theory) and covers boundary-value problems explicitly. Those are the right foundations for PDE / separation of variables, and later for tensors and differential geometry. That favours 2270 + 2280 for the app's "Mathematics of Spacetime" track.
- **The department may have changed its expectation.** The old page and the 2016 syllabus put MATH 2250 first. The current catalog names MATH 2280 as the highest requirement. This is an inference from documents of different dates.
- **Possible shortfall on the 2250 route.** Taking MATH 2250 may leave a student short of the stated 13 math credits. Whether the catalog then requires extra credit is unknown.

### Gaps
- **Exact Coursedog wording of the alternative.** For example, "MATH 2250 OR (MATH 2270 AND MATH 2280)". Also unknown: whether there is a credit-shortfall note, and whether USU restricts credit for both MATH 2250 and MATH 2270/2280.
- **MATH 2270's official catalog description and credit value.**
