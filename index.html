Projects

Different ideas on the daily. Eight browser toys, one per directory, sharing no code and no build step — each one runs as static files.

  • moon/ Selene

    An open-world exploration game on the real Moon, at its real size. Land anywhere, walk in a sixth of a gravity, and climb down into a lava-tube skylight. Every value on screen says whether it was measured, interpolated, derived or invented.

  • raptor/ Raptor

    An F-22A flight simulator with a six-degree-of-freedom flight model, fly-by-wire control laws, thrust vectoring, and a streamed world at true Earth scale.

  • universe/ Horizon

    An observable universe explorer. One continuous zoom from Earth to the edge of what can be seen.

  • supernova/ Supernova

    The death of a massive star: a red supergiant's final minutes, iron-core collapse, the neutrino burst, a lopsided shock revival, and three thousand years of remnant evolution.

  • blackhole/ Black Hole Explorer

    A real-time Kerr black hole, rendered by tracing null geodesics through curved spacetime on the GPU.

  • helios/ Helios

    A first-person spaceship flight sandbox through the real solar system.

  • branch/ Branch

    A music-driven generative lightning and vascular growth system.

  • propulsion/ Propulsion

    A two-semester-equivalent rocket propulsion engineering course: 36 modules across liquid bipropellant engines, solid motors and cold-gas thrusters, with exams, a capstone trade study and a verified engine database.

Source: github.com/Daxjdavis526/Projects. Desktop browsers, WebGL2.
